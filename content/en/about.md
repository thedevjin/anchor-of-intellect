# About the project

Anchor of Intellect is a long-term research project initiated by Jin Sung.

It grows from a continuing question: how can AI understand a person deeply, help them carry out meaningful work and still respect their direction and choices?

The work behind that question includes building Auxtho and exploring memory, delegated work, multi-AI collaboration, computer connections and active defense. A.O.I. gathers the long-term direction; it does not turn the different projects into one already completed system.

## Personal project, clear relationships

This project is intended to be maintained through Jin Sung's personal GitHub account. It is not presented as an independent foundation, a standards body or a consensus on behalf of other people.

Auxtho's company charter states company commitments. The Ardamire × WhiteCell defense charter concerns the design of protection and enforcement. The A.O.I. draft explores a broader long-term research direction. These scopes are related, but not interchangeable.

The initial site does not reproduce or relicense those company documents.

## How to read the charter

The charter describes aims and design commitments. Implemented behavior must be shown by separate, appropriately scoped evidence.

Where research has not settled a question, it should remain a question. Where a system has been tested only locally or in a limited setting, that limit should remain visible.

## A small beginning, an ambitious direction

The project begins with a charter and a transparent revision record. Further architecture and research should earn their place through substantive work, not empty roadmap headings.

The aim is not simply for AI to do more. It is for people to be able to become more fully themselves.
