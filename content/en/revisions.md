# Revisions

A record of what changes, why it changes, and who adopts it.

## 0.1 — Draft · 18 October 2026

The first bilingual project draft brings together human possibility, meaningful agency, memory, bounded delegation, independent enforcement and recovery.

This is a draft, not an adopted public release. No public consensus or institutional endorsement is claimed.

The Auxtho AI Charter v1.0 and Ardamire × WhiteCell Defense Charter v1.1 are separate documents. Their versions and publication decisions are not changed by this draft.

## French reading edition · 18 October 2026

A French reading edition is prepared alongside the original English and Korean draft. The principles and selected closing remain the same; language controls and revision procedures are extended to cover all three languages.

## How a revision becomes a version

A proposal should identify the passage, the problem, the proposed change and its consequences. Material changes must be considered in all maintained languages.

Jin Sung decides whether to adopt a change and records the reason. Automated checks can expose structural errors; they cannot adopt a principle. Publication remains a separate decision.

A released version should retain the English, Korean and French source texts, its date, source commit and change record. A correction should be visible as a correction, not silently substituted into history.

## Three languages, one intent

English is the default presentation language. It is not silently declared superior to Korean or French. If the texts materially differ, the discrepancy must be resolved and documented.
