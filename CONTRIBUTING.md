# Proposing a revision

This project is initiated and maintained by Jin Sung. Its charter starts as a proposal, not a claim of public consensus.

## A useful proposal

State the passage, the concrete problem, the proposed wording, and the consequences or tradeoffs. Distinguish evidence, interpretation and an open question. Do not include confidential information or personal data that you do not have permission to publish.

Where a change affects meaning, propose corresponding English, Korean and French revisions together. Minor translation corrections should still identify the source passage.

## Review and adoption

1. A proposal is discussed and checked against the charter's purpose.
2. A substantive change is considered for all maintained languages, including foreseeable misuse and who gains or loses agency.
3. Jin Sung explicitly adopts or declines the change and records a short rationale.
4. An adopted version receives its version, date and fixed source files.
5. Publication is a separate, explicitly approved action.

Automated checks validate structure and consistency; they do not confer moral authority or approve a change. Opening or merging a pull request is not, by itself, adoption of a charter or approval to publish a site.

No promise of response time, institutional endorsement or community governance is made. The process may be revised openly as the project develops.

Do not use issues or pull requests for vulnerability details or secrets.
